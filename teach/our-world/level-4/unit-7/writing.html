📝 Present: What is Fact & Opinion Writing?
Let's discover the writing type 🔍
📌 Fact & Opinion Writing — What Is It?
In fact and opinion writing, you state your opinion and use facts to support your opinion. An opinion is something you believe to be true. To introduce opinions, use words like in my opinion, I think, and I believe. A fact is a piece of true information, for example, a date, an event, or a name.

A Good Idea ✍️
📖 "A Good Idea" — Paragraph 1
"In my opinion, sticky notes 📎 are a great invention." They're easy to use. You write a note and stick it on your notebook or on your computer. And they come off easily. "I think most people like them because they help you to remember things."
📖 "A Good Idea" — Paragraph 2
"In my opinion, the story of sticky notes is interesting, too." Sticky notes have two inventors. Spencer Silver invented the glue in 1970. It wasn't strong, so he didn't know how to use it. But four years later, Arthur Fry found a use for it. One day, all his notes fell on the floor. He wasn't happy! Then he remembered Silver's glue! He used the glue on small pieces of paper. It worked! You could stick the notes to almost any surface, and it was easy to remove them. Now we have a cool—and useful ✅—invention!

Writing Support + Teaching Tips
✅ Writing Support: Pronouns Must Match! 🔤
A pronoun (he, she, it, they) replaces a noun. The pronoun must MATCH the noun it replaces in number (singular/plural) and gender.
✅ "Jared found his book." (Jared = he = his ✅)
✅ "Luisa opened her backpack." (Luisa = she = her ✅)
✅ "Jared and Luisa rode their bikes." (Jared + Luisa = they = their ✅)
🔧 Fix the wrong pronoun:
Alexander Sørloth scored a goal. She is a great striker. ✅ He — correct!
Mbappé and Kane are stars. It plays for European clubs. ✅ They — correct!
The invention was new. They changed everything. ✅ It — correct!
👨‍🏫 Teaching Tip for Dad
After Leo writes his draft, ask him to underline every pronoun (he, she, it, they, his, her, its, their). Then ask: "What noun does this pronoun replace?"

If the noun is singular (one person or thing) — the pronoun must be singular too.
If it's plural — the pronoun must be plural.

This takes 5 minutes and prevents the most common writing error at this level.
⭐ Dad Tip: Pronoun errors are easy to miss when reading fast. Slow down and check one sentence at a time.
