🌍 BE THE EXPERT — About the Photo
Lion Lights 🦁💡 — Richard Turere
🦁 Lion Lights — Nairobi, Kenya

Richard Turere was just a young boy in Kenya. His father kept livestock — cows, goats, and sheep. But lions from Nairobi National Park attacked the animals at night.

The villagers killed the lions to protect their animals. Both the lions AND the livestock were in danger!

In 2012, Richard had an idea. He invented Lion Lights — solar-powered lights that flash and flicker at night. The lions think someone is moving with a torch — so they stay away! 🦁

✅ Livestock safe ✅ Lions safe 🌍 Used worldwide

❓ Discussion Questions

  • What problems did Richard solve?
  • How old was Richard when he invented Lion Lights?
  • Is Lion Lights a good invention? Why?
  • Where else are Lion Lights used?
