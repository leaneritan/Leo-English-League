curiosity 🔍

typical 📋
Normal, usual, or happening often.
Dad 💬 "A typical problem in our house is losing the TV remote."
Leo 💬 "A typical problem at school is forgetting my homework!"
Typical means something that usually happens — it's normal, everyday, and common.

Think
Use your imagination & creativity
Ask Leo to think of 2-3 typical problems he faces. Guide with examples if needed. Soccer tip: Think of problems ON the pitch — goalkeeper not seeing the ball, players faking injuries, bad pitch conditions. These are typical problems that technology has helped solve!

Think — Question 1 🤔

What are some typical problems in your daily life?
Dad 💬 "A typical problem for me: I always lose my keys in the morning!"
Leo 💬 "A typical problem for me: my phone battery always dies right before a football match!"
Problem starters:
💭 "I always forget..."
🔍 "I can never find..."
😤 "It is annoying when..."
🏫 "A typical problem at school..."
Help Leo connect a problem from Q1 to an invention idea. It doesn't have to be technically realistic — imagination is the goal! Model: "My keys problem → I'd invent a smart tag that beeps when I clap."

Think — Question 2 💡

Can you solve any of them with an invention? 🔧
Think about your problem from Question 1. Now imagine an invention to solve it!
Dad 💬 "My problem: losing keys. My invention: a smart chip in my keyring that beeps when I clap 👏"
Leo 💬 "My problem: phone dying at matches. My invention: a wristband that charges my phone using my body heat — like Ann Makosinski's flashlight! ⚽🔋"
Invention starters:
🔧 "I would invent a device that..."
⚙️ "My invention would use..."
✅ "This would solve the problem because..."
💡 Remember:
All great inventions started as imagination! The Popsicle was an accident. Sheep View 360 used animals. Be creative!
Help Leo evaluate his invention idea. Is it easy or difficult to build? What materials would you need? This prepares him for the Pair discussion. Model: "My key-finder is relatively easy — the technology already exists in Tile trackers!"

Share
Which invention is best?
Leo presents his invention idea as if pitching it to a class. You play "the class" and ask one follow-up question. Then discuss together which invention idea — Leo's, or one of the ones from the reading — you both liked best and why.

Share — Present Your Invention 🎤

Present your invention idea. Use this structure:
1️⃣ The problem: "A typical problem is..."
2️⃣ My invention: "My invention is..."
3️⃣ How it works: "It works by..."
4️⃣ Why it's useful: "This invention is useful because..."
Dad's follow-up questions:
❓ "How much would this cost to make?"
❓ "Who would use this invention?"
❓ "What problem does it solve better than existing inventions?"
❓ "Would you name it? What would you call it?"
Both Dad and Leo vote for their favourite invention — it can be from the reading, from Ozcan, from Leo's idea, or any other invention they discussed. After voting, ask: "Why did you choose that one?" If Leo's own invention wins, celebrate!
