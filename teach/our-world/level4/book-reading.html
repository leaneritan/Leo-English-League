⚡ Reading Strategy — Cause and Effect
Cause and Effect ⚡ ➡️ ✅
⚡
CAUSE
WHY did it happen?
The reason. The first thing.
Signal words:
because · since · as
✅
EFFECT
WHAT happened?
The result. What changed.
Signal words:
so · therefore · as a result

🏨 Setting the scene…

Ruth Wakefield had a small hotel. She had many guests. She made cookies for them every day — she followed a recipe.

🍫 The accident begins…

One day, she put pieces of a chocolate bar into her cookie dough. She expected the chocolate to melt in the oven and become a chocolate cookie.

💥 The SURPRISE!

But the chocolate did NOT melt! It stayed as small, crispy chips. It was an accident! She did not plan this at all.

🍪 The result!

The guests loved the cookies! The chocolate chip cookie was born. Today it is one of the most popular snacks in the world!

Q1: Who invented the chocolate chip cookie? What was she doing? ▼
Ruth Wakefield invented the chocolate chip cookie. She was following a recipe and making cookies for the guests at her hotel.
Q2: What did Ruth expect to happen? What actually happened? ▼
She expected the chocolate to melt and make a chocolate cookie. But the chocolate did NOT melt — it stayed crispy! It was an accident.
Q3: How old was Frank Epperson when he invented the popsicle? ▼
Frank was 11 years old. It was the year 1905. He used a stick to stir a sweet powder drink and left it outside in the cold by accident.
Q4: What is one thing the two inventions have in common? ▼
Both were invented by accident — nobody planned them! Both used simple, everyday ingredients. Both became extremely popular snacks around the world.
Q5: What is the main idea of this book? ▼
The main idea is that some of the best and most popular food inventions in the world happened by accident — not by careful planning!
🔍 Compare & Contrast + Grammar in Context
Compare the Two Inventions

🍪 CHOCOLATE CHIP COOKIE vs 🍦 POPSICLE

  • Same: Both were accidents. Both used simple ingredients. Both became popular snacks.
  • Different: Cookie → heat (oven). Popsicle → cold (outside).
  • Different: Ruth was an adult. Frank was 11 years old!
  • Different: Cookie was at a hotel. Popsicle was sold at a fair.
💡 Conclusion: You do not need to plan to be a great inventor! Sometimes accidents create the best things.
