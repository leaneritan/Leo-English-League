🔧 Rule 1 · Affirmative

Talking about past habits

subject + used to + base verb
I used to ride a bike to school. 🚲
We used to watch cartoons on Saturdays. 📺
Kvaratskhelia used to play for Napoli. ⚽🇮🇹
📌
used to is the same for I · you · he · she · we · they — you never change it! ✨
🔧 Rule 2 · Negative

Something that did NOT happen

subject + didn't use to + base verb
People didn't use to have airplanes. ✈️❌
Cherki didn't use to play in the Premier League. 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿❌
We didn't use to have the internet. 🌐❌
⚠️
In the negative, used loses its -d → use to

🧠 Be the Expert · Grammar in Depth

Why do we use used to?

1
We use used to to contrast a past situation with NOW.
2
Long period: I used to have long hair. Now I have short hair. 💇
3
Repeated action: I used to walk to school. Now I ride my bike. 🚲
4
Join with but → I used to walk to school, but now I ride my bike.
⚠️ Common Mistakes

Be careful — don't do this!

❌ I used to played soccer.
→
✅ I used to play soccer. ⚽
❌ I didn't used to have a phone.
→
✅ I didn't use to have a phone. 📵
❌ Did you used to live here?
→
✅ Did you use to live here? 🏠
🎯 Golden rule: after did or didn't → no -d on "use"!
🔍 Don't Mix These Up

Used to vs usually

used to
PAST habit (no longer true)
"I used to play with Lego." 🧱
⏪ then ≠ now
usually
habit NOW (still happening)
"I usually play soccer on Saturdays." ⚽
🔁 still happening today
🎯 Try this: Kvaratskhelia used to play for Napoli, but now he plays for PSG. ⚽
