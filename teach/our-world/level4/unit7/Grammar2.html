📐 Grammar in Depth
1
Use YOU for instructions everyone can follow
When you write instructions or directions, YOU means anyone reading this.
Example: "You need to have creativity to invent things." 💡
🎯 Leo — can you make your own example?
📐 Grammar in Depth
2
Use YOU to describe how inventions work
To explain what an invention does, use YOU to show how anyone uses it.
Example: "You cut meat with it. You hold it in your hand." 🔪
🎯 How do you use a microwave? 🍳
📐 Grammar in Depth
3
Use YOU for general facts and advice
When you give advice or state a universal fact, YOU refers to everyone.
Example: "You should always try again if you fail." 💪
🎯 Give Dad one piece of advice using YOU!
📐 Grammar in Depth
4
Questions also use general YOU
When asking how something works, YOU in the question means everyone.
Example: "What do you do with this invention?" 🤔
"How do you use it?" 🔍
🎯 Ask Dad: "How do you use a ___?" (pick any object!)

🔁 Recap
What we know about general YOU

🎯 YOU (specific) = talking to ONE person directly
🧮 Formula: YOU + VERB + with it / in it / on it
❓ Questions too: "How do you use it?" = asking everyone
