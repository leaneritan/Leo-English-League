Be the Expert
Giving Good Feedback
Read the sentence starters together. Then model: Dad gives Leo feedback on HIS app using these starters. Show how good feedback is honest but kind — it helps make the idea BETTER, not worse. Leo should then use these when he "presents" his app.

How to Give Good Feedback 💬

Good feedback is honest and kind. It makes the idea better!
✅ "Your app looks fun. However, I don't understand how it will..."
✅ "I like your idea, but I would maybe change..."
✅ "The ideas are great, but I think you don't need..."
✅ "I really like the part where..."
⚽ Football connection: After every UCL match, coaches give players feedback — what was great AND what to improve. That's how Raphinha scored 13 goals this season! Always honest, always kind.
❌ Avoid this:
"This is a bad idea."
"That will never work."
"This is boring."
These don't help anyone improve. Always explain WHY and HOW to make it better.
