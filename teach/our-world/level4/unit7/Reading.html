Reading Strategy: Ask Questions ❓

Good readers ask questions while they read.

Before: Who is this about? Why is it important?

While reading: Who? What? Where? When? Why? How?

After: What did I learn? What was unclear?
📖 You read: "Angad suffered from asthma 😮‍💨."
❓ You ask: "What is asthma? Why did he get it?"
💡 Asking questions helps you understand and remember!

Paragraph 2 — 2018: Angad Daryani 🔧

2018
At the age of 19, Angad Daryani invented a solution to the problem of air pollution 🌫️🌫️ harmful substances in the air that damage health in cities.
Growing up in Mumbai, India, he suffered from asthma 😮‍💨😮‍💨 a health problem that makes it hard to breathe caused by the polluted air.
Air pollution 🌫️ is a big problem.
Three million 🔢🔢 a very large number — 1,000,000 or more people die each year.
6.1 meters = about as tall as a basketball hoop + a child on top.

Paragraph 2 — Angad's Invention 🏗️

Angad's invention 🔧 is an air pollution cleaning tower that is 6.1 meters (20 ft.) high.
The tower sucks in air and takes out the particles of dust and carbon.
The particles are collected and can even be reused.
Angad's dream is to build thousands of the towers and improve the quality of air in cities.
Model: Angad wanted to solve air pollution. He built a 6.1m tower that sucks in air and removes dust and carbon particles.

Question: Paragraph 2 ❓

What problem did Angad want to solve with his invention?
✅ Model answer: "Angad wanted to solve the problem of air pollution 🌫️ in cities. He built a 6.1 meter tower that sucks in air and removes dust and carbon particles."
The flashlight is "hollow" because body heat passes through a hollow tube to generate electricity.

Paragraph 3 — 2013: Ann Makosinski 🔦

2013
Canadian high school student Ann Makosinski was 15 when she invented the "hollow flashlight."
The flashlight uses heat from the human body and changes it into light.
It doesn't need batteries 🔋🔋 small containers of stored energy.
Ann thought of the idea when she heard about a friend who lived in the Philippines.
1.1 billion = 1,100,000,000 people — about 14% of the world's population.

Paragraph 3 — Ann's Mission 🌍

Her friend was doing badly in school.
She needed to do her homework at night, but there was no electrical light in her home.
Ann's flashlight isn't expensive.
Ann hopes it will help some of the 1.1 billion people in the world who still have no electricity ⚡⚡ the power that makes lights and machines work in their homes.
Emphasise: Ann's invention was inspired by wanting to help someone else.

Question: Paragraph 3 ❓

How did Ann come up with the idea for her invention?
✅ Model answer: "Ann heard about a friend in the Philippines who couldn't do her homework at night because there was no electricity ⚡ in her home. Ann wanted to help people without electricity."

Paragraph 5 — 1824: Louis Braille 📖

1824
Louis Braille had an accident when he was three.
The accident left him blind 👁️👁️ not able to see.
At that time, it was hard for blind children to read.
They had to touch raised letters.
Braille uses patterns of up to 6 raised dots. Each pattern is a different letter.

Paragraph 5 — The Braille Alphabet ⠃⠗⠁⠊⠇⠇⠑

But it was easy to confuse a Q with an O, an R with a B, and so on.
When he was fifteen, Louis invented an alphabet that used raised dots.
The Braille alphabet was a big success! 🎉

Question: Paragraph 5 ❓
