All the rules
5 rules
01
Regular — add -er
Most short adjectives. Just add -er to the end.
Examples
tall→taller
old→older
short→shorter
fast→faster
cheap→cheaper
In a sentence
My dad is older than my mum.
The train is faster than the bus.
02
Words ending in -y — change y to i, add -er
Drop the y, write i, then add -er.
Examples
friendly→friendlier
easy→easier
happy→happier
busy→busier
pretty→prettier
In a sentence
Math is easier than science for me.
I'm happier in summer than in winter.
03
Short words with one vowel — double the last letter
If the word ends in one short vowel + one consonant, double the consonant before -er.
Examples
big→bigger
hot→hotter
thin→thinner
sad→sadder
fat→fatter
In a sentence
Egypt is hotter than Canada.
Elephants are bigger than horses.
04
Long words — use "more" instead of -er
For words with two or more syllables, use more in front of the adjective.
Examples
beautiful→more beautiful
interesting→more interesting
difficult→more difficult
expensive→more expensive
In a sentence
Soccer is more exciting than golf.
This book is more interesting than that one.
05
Irregular — memorize these
These don't follow any rule. You just have to remember them.
Examples
good→better
bad→worse
far→farther / further
little→less
much / many→more
In a sentence
My English is better than last year.
Today's weather is worse than yesterday's.
10 mixed examples
Real-world
In the wild — comparatives mixed together. You'll find -er words, more words, and irregulars side by side, the way you'd read them in a book or hear them in a conversation.
01
My new phone is more expensive than my old one. Rule 4 · "more"
02
I feel happier on weekends than on school days. Rule 2 · y → i
03
Cheetahs run faster than any other land animal. Rule 1 · regular
04
My little brother draws better than me. Rule 5 · irregular
05
The weather in Tokyo is hotter in summer than in Osaka. Rule 3 · doubling
06
Science class is more difficult than English class this year. Rule 4 · "more"
07
This road is longer than the one we took last time. Rule 1 · regular
08
Today is worse than yesterday — it's raining again. Rule 5 · irregular
09
My cat is friendlier than most dogs I've met. Rule 2 · y → i
10
The new stadium is bigger and more modern than the old one. Rule 3 + Rule 4
