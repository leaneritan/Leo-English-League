🧩 Strategy — Imperatives for Instructions

📌 Imperatives for Instructions
Use the base form of the verb — no subject (no I / you / he / she...).
Use sequence words: First → Next → Then → Finally
This makes instructions clear and polite!
❌ "You put the hoop on the ground."
✅ "Put the hoop on the ground."
❌ "I lift my arms to celebrate."
✅ "Lift your arms to celebrate!"
❌ "We move to the left side."
✅ "Move to the left side."
