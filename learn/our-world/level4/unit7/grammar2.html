📦 "You" for General Statements

🔑 The Key Pattern
YOU
+
VERB
+
with / in / on it
HOW DO YOU USE IT?
→
YOU + verb + with it

🧠 Grammar in Depth

Rule 1
Use "you" for instructions — anyone can follow them
🔄 "You put the hula hoop on the ground. You lift it to your waist."
Rule 2
Use "you" to describe how inventions work
🔪 "You cut meat with it. You hold it in your hand."
Rule 3
Use "you" for general facts and advice
💡 "You need creativity to invent things." / "You should always try again."
Rule 4
Questions also use general "you"
🗣️ "How do you use it?" = How do PEOPLE use it?
